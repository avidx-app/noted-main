## Quick context

Kai is the most AI-native user in the set. He actively uses AI settings, switches providers, experiments with Coworker and Squad, and cares about why one model or prompt path produced a given result. He is useful because he validates the real strength of Noted's current live AI surface while also exposing where power-user workflow clarity still falls short.
