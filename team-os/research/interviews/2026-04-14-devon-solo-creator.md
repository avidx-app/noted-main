## Quick context

Devon runs a one-person business selling cohort-based writing classes. He uses Noted heavily for idea generation, lesson planning, and first-draft assembly. He is one of the clearest examples of the chat-to-publish gap: high Coworker usage, regular document creation, almost no publish behavior from inside the product.
