## Quick context

Jordan is an MBA student using Noted for class notes, reading summaries, interview prep, and side-project planning. Jordan is less sophisticated than Kai and less team-operational than Anya, which makes the product's onboarding strengths and weaknesses easier to see. The clearest pattern here is that live AI features deliver immediate value, while anything that asks the user to design a system up front creates drop-off.
