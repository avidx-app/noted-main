## Account context

Ethan is a junior product analyst using Noted for course notes, interview prep, and side-project planning. He is active enough to be beyond first-week novelty but still early enough that onboarding friction is easy to observe. This call focused on activation: what clicked fast, what stalled, and what kept the product from becoming more central.
