# Mia — publish friction / draft review gap

## Account context

Mia is an operations manager at a small remote agency. She is already an active user with recurring Coworker usage, mostly for turning internal notes into cleaner project updates and handoff docs. This was a CS-style call to understand why strong drafting activity was not converting into stronger publishing or team circulation behavior.

## Transcript

**[00:12] Mia:** Good instinct. I am definitely using the product. I just do not think publish is the right next step most of the time.

**[00:20] Priya:** What is the right next step most of the time?

**[00:23] Mia:** One other person seeing the draft before it becomes a real artifact.

**[00:29] Priya:** Internal review, basically.
