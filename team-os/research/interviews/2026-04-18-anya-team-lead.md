## Quick context

Anya leads a four-person content and partnerships team at a B2B startup. She already uses Noted herself for first drafts, partner briefs, and strategy memos, mainly through `/Ask AI`, Coworker, and the cleaner editor flow. Her core tension is not with a broken collaboration feature. It is that the current product works well enough for solo drafting that she wants to bring more work into it, but the only outward step today is `publish-to-web`, which is the wrong shape for internal review.
